What Internet Speed Do I Need?

Use this guide to determine the right speed for your household

Activity Minimum Speed Recommended
Email & Web Browsing 1-5 Mbps 10+ Mbps
Social Media 3-5 Mbps 10+ Mbps
HD Video Streaming (Netflix, etc.) 5 Mbps 25+ Mbps
4K Video Streaming 25 Mbps 50+ Mbps
Video Conferencing (Zoom) 3 Mbps 25+ Mbps
Online Gaming 5 Mbps 50+ Mbps
Working From Home 25 Mbps 100+ Mbps
Smart Home Devices 5-10 Mbps per device 100+ Mbps total

Pro Tip: Multiply the speeds by the number of people/devices using the internet simultaneously. A family of 4 with multiple devices should aim for at least 200 Mbps.

Connection Types Explained

Understanding the differences between fiber, cable, DSL, and 5G

Fiber Optic

Best for: Heavy users, gamers, large households

  • Fastest speeds (up to 5 Gbps)
  • Symmetrical upload/download
  • Most reliable connection
  • Low latency for gaming

Providers: AT&T Fiber, Frontier, altafiber

Cable Internet

Best for: Most households, streaming, general use

  • Fast speeds (up to 1 Gbps)
  • Widely available
  • Good for streaming
  • Slower upload speeds

Providers: Spectrum, Optimum

DSL

Best for: Light users, rural areas

  • Moderate speeds (up to 100 Mbps)
  • Uses phone lines
  • Available in most areas
  • Speed depends on distance

Providers: Windstream, EarthLink

5G / Fixed Wireless

Best for: Urban areas, flexibility, no wiring needed

  • Speeds up to 300 Mbps
  • No installation required
  • No contracts
  • Requires 5G coverage

Providers: T-Mobile 5G Home Internet

How to Save on Your Internet Bill

Call your provider and ask for a better rate. Mention competitor pricing. Many providers have retention departments that can offer promotional rates to keep you as a customer.
Many providers charge $10-15/month to rent equipment. Buying your own can save $100+ per year. Make sure to check compatibility with your provider first.
A household of 2 people probably doesn't need gigabit internet. Use our speed guide above to find the right plan for your actual usage.
Some providers offer discounts when you bundle internet with TV or mobile phone service. But always compare the total cost vs. buying services separately.
New customer rates are often 30-50% lower than regular pricing. If you're switching providers, you can take advantage of these deals.
