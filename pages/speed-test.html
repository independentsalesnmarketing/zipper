Improve Your Speed Test Accuracy

Get the most reliable results with these tips

Use a Wired Connection

Connect directly to your router with an ethernet cable for the most accurate results.

Close Other Apps

Streaming, downloads, and background apps consume bandwidth and skew results.

Test Multiple Times

Run 3–5 tests at different times of day, then average the results.
