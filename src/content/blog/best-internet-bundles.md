## Are Internet + TV Bundles Still Worth It in 2026?

The short answer: **usually not.** In most cases, buying internet-only and adding a streaming live TV service (like YouTube TV) costs less than a traditional cable TV bundle. But there are exceptions — and some households genuinely benefit from bundling.

Let's break it down.

## Traditional Bundles vs Internet + Streaming

### The Math: Traditional Bundle
| Component | Typical Cost |
|----------|-------------|
| Internet + TV bundle | $110-150/mo |
| DVR fee | $10-20/mo |
| HD fee | $10/mo |
| Broadcast/regional sports fee | $15-25/mo |
| Box rental fee (per TV) | $10-15/mo per TV |
| **True monthly cost** | **$155-225/mo** |

### The Math: Internet-Only + Streaming
| Component | Typical Cost |
|----------|-------------|
| Internet only (300 Mbps) | $50/mo |
| YouTube TV (live cable channels) | $73/mo |
| **True monthly cost** | **$123/mo** |

**Internet + streaming saves $30-100/mo** compared to traditional cable bundles when you factor in all the hidden fees that make cable TV bills bloat.

## When Traditional Bundles Make Sense

Despite the math usually favoring internet + streaming, bundles win in these scenarios:

1. **You need regional sports networks (RSNs)** — If your local NBA, MLB, or NHL team is on a regional sports network, traditional cable or DirecTV Stream may be the only way to watch. YouTube TV has dropped many RSNs.

2. **Multiple TVs need live TV** — Cable offers unlimited TV hookups (with box fees). Streaming services limit simultaneous streams to 3-5.

3. **You get an employer/association discount** — Some employers or organizations offer discounted cable TV bundles that make the math competitive.

4. **Senior household preferences** — Some seniors prefer the familiar cable TV interface over navigating streaming apps.

## Tips for Getting the Best Bundle Deal

### 1. Always Price Out Internet-Only First
Before accepting a bundle, calculate internet-only + streaming costs. More often than not, this approach wins.

### 2. Negotiate at Renewal Time
When your bundle promo expires, contact support to negotiate. Say: "I'm considering internet-only with YouTube TV. Can you offer a better TV rate?" Retention departments often have unadvertised deals.

### 3. Watch for Hidden Fees
Traditional TV bundles advertise one price but charge extras. Always ask: "What will my total monthly bill actually be, including ALL fees, taxes, and equipment?"

### 4. Consider Seasonal Bundles for Sports
If you only want live TV for football or basketball season, subscribe to YouTube TV or Hulu Live for those months and cancel when the season ends. No-contract streaming lets you do this.
