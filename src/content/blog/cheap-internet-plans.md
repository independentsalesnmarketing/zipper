## Can You Really Get Internet for Under $30/Month?

Yes — but with caveats. The lowest-cost internet plans typically come from:
1. **Low-income assistance programs** (best value, if you qualify)
2. **Basic DSL tiers** (slow but cheap)
3. **Promotional first-year pricing** (cheap initially, then increases)
4. **Prepaid/mobile hotspot plans** (limited data)

Here's what's available in each category.

### How to Apply
1. Check if you receive qualifying benefits (SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, WIC, free school lunch)
2. Visit the provider's website and search for their low-income program
3. Provide proof of eligibility (benefit card, enrollment letter)
4. Service is typically activated within 3-5 business days

### Lifeline Program
The federal **Lifeline Program** provides a **$9.25/month discount** on broadband service. It's available to qualifying low-income households. Apply at [lifelinesupport.org](https://www.lifelinesupport.org/).

## How to Lower Your Existing Internet Bill

Already paying too much? Try these strategies:

### 1. Call and Negotiate ($0 effort, potential $20-40/mo savings)
Call your provider and say: "I've been a customer for X years. My bill has increased and I'm considering switching to [competitor]. Can you offer me a better rate?"

**Key phrases that work**:
- "I'd like to speak with the retention department"
- "What promotions are available for existing customers?"
- "Can you match [competitor's current new-customer offer]?"

### 2. Downgrade Your Plan ($10-40/mo savings)
Most households use far less bandwidth than they pay for. If you're paying for 1 Gbps but only use 50-150 Mbps, downgrading to a 300 Mbps plan saves significant money.

Run a [speed test](/speed-test) during your typical usage to see how much bandwidth you actually use.

### 3. Drop Equipment Rental ($5-15/mo savings)
Stop renting your provider's modem and router. Buy compatible equipment outright:
- **DOCSIS 3.1 modem**: $70-100
- **WiFi 6 router**: $80-150
- **Break-even**: 5-10 months

### 4. Drop the Bundle
If you have a TV+internet bundle, calculate whether internet-only + streaming services would cost less. In most cases, it does.

### 5. Switch Providers
Competition keeps prices low. If a competitor offers a better deal, switch. With no-contract plans from most providers, there's no penalty for leaving.
