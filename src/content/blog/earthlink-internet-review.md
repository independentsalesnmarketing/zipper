## EarthLink Internet Overview

EarthLink is a unique player in the broadband market. Unlike ISPs that own their own cable or fiber infrastructure, EarthLink operates as a **reseller** — partnering with local providers to deliver fiber, cable, and fixed wireless internet under the EarthLink brand. The result is wider coverage than most fiber-focused ISPs, plus a strong privacy and customer service focus.

## EarthLink Plans & Pricing

### Fiber Plans
EarthLink's fiber plans use the fiber networks of providers like AT&T, Frontier, and CenturyLink:

- **300 Mbps** — $49.99/mo
- **500 Mbps** — $59.99/mo
- **1 Gbps** — $69.99/mo
- **2 Gbps** — $99.99/mo
- **5 Gbps** — $149.99/mo

All fiber plans include symmetrical upload speeds and no data cap.

### Wireless Home Internet
For areas without fiber:

- **50 Mbps** — $49.99/mo
- **100 Mbps** — $59.99/mo

Wireless plans use T-Mobile or other carrier networks.

## Why Choose EarthLink Over the Direct Provider?

Fair question. If EarthLink resells AT&T Fiber, why not just get AT&T directly? A few reasons:

1. **Customer service** — EarthLink consistently ranks higher in customer satisfaction than many infrastructure providers. You deal with EarthLink's support team, not the underlying carrier's.

2. **Privacy focus** — EarthLink has a strong privacy policy and doesn't sell your browsing data. They even offer a built-in security suite.

3. **Single billing** — If you move between areas served by different underlying providers, EarthLink offers continuous service and a single account.

4. **Competitive pricing** — EarthLink's rates are often equal to or lower than going direct, especially on fiber plans.

## Real-World Performance

Because EarthLink uses partner networks, performance mirrors the underlying provider:

- **On AT&T Fiber network**: Expect 95%+ of advertised speeds, symmetrical upload
- **On Frontier Fiber**: Similar 95%+ performance, symmetrical
- **On wireless networks**: Variable, typically 60-85% of advertised speeds

The infrastructure quality depends on which provider serves your address. EarthLink fiber = same fiber, different customer experience.

## What We Like

- **Broad availability** — Coverage through multiple partner networks reaches more addresses than any single fiber provider
- **Strong privacy stance** — Doesn't sell browsing data, includes security features
- **No contracts** — Month-to-month service on all plans
- **Customer service** — U.S.-based support with higher satisfaction ratings than many competitors
- **Straightforward pricing** — No hidden fees or dramatic post-promo increases
- **No data caps on fiber** — Unlimited usage on all fiber plans

## What Could Be Better

- **You're not the direct customer** — If there's a physical line issue, EarthLink has to coordinate with the underlying provider, which can slow resolution
- **Wireless plans have data considerations** — The wireless home internet tier may have deprioritization during congestion
- **Limited brand awareness** — Many people don't realize EarthLink still exists (they've been around since 1994)
- **Equipment varies** — What modem/router you get depends on the underlying network
- **No TV bundles** — Internet-only, no cable TV option

## Who Should Choose EarthLink?

**EarthLink is good for:**
- People who value privacy and don't want their ISP selling their data
- Anyone frustrated with their current provider's customer service
- Households in areas where EarthLink fiber pricing beats the direct provider
- People wanting a single provider relationship that works across multiple areas

**Look elsewhere if:**
- You prefer dealing directly with the infrastructure owner for faster support
- Frontier or AT&T Fiber is cheaper in your area
- You need the absolute cheapest option available
