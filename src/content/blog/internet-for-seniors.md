## What Seniors Need From Internet Service

The best internet for seniors isn't about having the fastest speeds — it's about **reliability, simplicity, and value**. After reviewing dozens of plans, here's what actually matters:

- **Reliable connection** — No random outages or speed drops
- **Simple setup and management** — Minimal technical headaches
- **Affordable pricing** — Fair rates that don't spike unexpectedly
- **Adequate speed** — 50-200 Mbps handles everything most seniors need
- **Good customer support** — Helpful, patient support when issues arise

## What Speed Do Seniors Actually Need?

You don't need gigabit internet. Here's a realistic guide:

| Usage | Speed Needed | Recommended Plan |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Email, news, social media | 25 Mbps | Any plan |
| Video calls (Zoom, FaceTime) | 25-50 Mbps | Any plan |
| Streaming Netflix/YouTube | 50-100 Mbps | Any plan |
| Multiple users streaming + video calls | 100-200 Mbps | Most entry plans |
| Smart home devices + streaming | 200-300 Mbps | Entry-level plans |

**The bottom line**: 100-300 Mbps handles everything a senior household needs. Don't let anyone sell you a 1 Gbps plan — it's overkill.

## Tips for Choosing Internet as a Senior

### 1. Ask About Professional Installation
If you're not tech-savvy, choose a provider that offers free professional installation. AT&T Fiber, Spectrum, and Verizon Fios all include this option.

### 2. Consider a Mesh WiFi System
If your home has dead spots, ask your provider about mesh WiFi add-ons. These extend coverage throughout your home so you get signal in every room.

### 3. Skip the TV Bundle
Internet-only plans are cheaper than bundled TV+internet packages. If you watch TV, a streaming service like YouTube TV ($73/mo) often costs less than a cable TV add-on.

### 4. Set Up Auto-Pay
Most providers offer $5-10/mo discounts for auto-pay. It also means one less bill to remember each month.

### 5. Get Help From Family or Friends
If possible, have a tech-comfortable family member or friend help you:
- Choose the right plan
- Set up your router and WiFi password
- Connect your devices
- Bookmark important websites
