## Spectrum Internet at a Glance

Spectrum (Charter Communications) is the second-largest cable internet provider in the U.S., serving 41 states. It's known for **no data caps, no annual contracts**, and straightforward pricing — a rare combination in the broadband industry.

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Technology | Cable (DOCSIS 3.1) |
| Speed Range | 300 Mbps – 1 Gbps |
| Starting Price | $49.99/mo |
| Contract Required | No |
| Data Cap | None |
| Equipment Fee | $5/mo (router) or free with modem |

## Spectrum Plans & Pricing

Spectrum keeps it simple with three tiers:

### Spectrum Internet — $49.99/mo
- **300 Mbps** download / 10 Mbps upload
- Best for: Households of 1-3 people, casual streaming, web browsing
- Includes free modem, $5/mo for WiFi router

### Spectrum Internet Ultra — $69.99/mo
- **500 Mbps** download / 20 Mbps upload
- Best for: Families of 3-5, multiple 4K streams, gaming
- Includes free modem, $5/mo for WiFi router

### Spectrum Internet Gig — $89.99/mo
- **1 Gbps** download / 35 Mbps upload
- Best for: Large households, work-from-home, heavy uploading
- Includes free modem, $5/mo for advanced WiFi 6E router

**Pro tip**: Skip Spectrum's router rental. Buy a compatible DOCSIS 3.1 modem and WiFi 6 router — it pays for itself in under a year.

## Real-World Speed Performance

In our testing, Spectrum consistently delivers **85-95% of advertised download speeds** during off-peak hours. During peak evening hours (7-11 PM), expect around 70-85% — still very usable.

Upload speeds are Spectrum's weak point. The 10-35 Mbps upload range is adequate for video calls and basic cloud backup, but if you're a content creator or regularly upload large files, you'll feel the limitation.

## What We Like

- **No data caps** — Stream, download, and game without worrying about overages
- **No contracts** — Cancel anytime without early termination fees
- **Transparent pricing** — Price increases are gradual, not the dramatic post-promo jumps other providers pull
- **Wide availability** — Available in 41 states across urban and suburban areas
- **Free modem** — Most cable providers charge $10-15/mo for modem rental

## What Could Be Better

- **Upload speeds lag behind fiber** — 10-35 Mbps upload vs 300-1000 Mbps from fiber providers
- **Price not the cheapest** — T-Mobile and some regional providers offer lower entry-level pricing
- **Promo pricing expires** — Rates increase $5-10/mo after 12 months
- **No bundled TV value** — Spectrum's TV add-on is expensive compared to streaming alternatives

## Who Should Choose Spectrum?

**Spectrum is ideal for:**
- Households where fiber isn't available (it covers areas fiber doesn't yet reach)
- People who hate contracts and data caps
- Moderate internet users who stream, browse, and game casually
- Families who want simple, reliable broadband without overthinking plans

**Look elsewhere if:**
- You need fast upload speeds (work-from-home power users, streamers)
- Fiber is available at your address for a similar price
- You're on a tight budget (T-Mobile 5G Home starts lower)
